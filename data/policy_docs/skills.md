# Learning & Development Catalog

All employees receive an annual learning stipend, subject to manager approval and relevance to role,
covering courses, certifications, books, or conference attendance.

Internal learning platform includes self-paced courses in data analysis, cloud fundamentals, product
management, and communication skills. New courses are added quarterly based on employee requests
submitted via the L&D portal.

Employees are encouraged to spend up to 2 hours per week during work hours on approved learning activities,
tracked informally through a personal development plan template available on the intranet.

The company runs an internal mentorship matching program - both as a mentor and mentee - open to all
employees regardless of tenure, matched based on stated skill and career interests via a short questionnaire.

Monthly "Lunch & Learn" sessions are hosted by internal subject-matter experts across departments, covering
everything from technical deep-dives to soft-skills topics like negotiation and public speaking; recordings
are archived on the internal learning platform for anyone who misses the live session.

Employees pursuing an external degree or professional certification relevant to their role (e.g. an MBA,
a PMP certification, a cloud architecture certification) can apply for tuition reimbursement, subject to
a minimum post-completion commitment period with the company.

Cross-functional "shadow days" let employees spend a day observing a different team's workflow, useful for
understanding adjacent functions before considering a lateral move or simply broadening perspective.
