# Financial Wellbeing Policy

ANCHOR Corp offers a cost-of-living adjustment reviewed annually every April, based on regional inflation data
and location-specific benchmarks. Adjustments are applied automatically and do not require an employee request.

Employees experiencing financial hardship can request an advance on earned wages through the HR portal,
capped at 50% of one month's net salary, repayable over 3 months with no interest. This does not affect
performance reviews or standing with the company and is treated as strictly confidential.

The Employee Assistance Program includes free confidential financial counseling sessions - up to 4 per year,
booked through the internal wellbeing portal, covering topics like budgeting, debt management, and
retirement planning.

Salary bands are reviewed every 12 months. Employees can request a compensation review meeting with their
manager at any time; managers are required to respond within 10 business days of any such request.

The company offers a Flexible Benefits Wallet, letting employees allocate a portion of their benefits budget
toward transportation, meal vouchers, childcare support, or additional health coverage based on personal need.

Employees relocating for work are eligible for a one-time relocation stipend, with the amount depending on
distance and whether the move is domestic or international - details are available from the HR mobility team.

For employees facing unexpected financial emergencies (medical, family crisis, natural disaster), the
Employee Hardship Fund provides need-based grants that do not need to be repaid, reviewed case-by-case
by a confidential committee separate from an employee's direct management chain.
