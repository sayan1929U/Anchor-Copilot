# Deloitte 2026 Gen Z & Millennial Survey — Financial Pressure Findings

According to Deloitte's 2026 Global Gen Z and Millennial Survey (22,595 respondents, 44 countries), cost of
living remains the single biggest concern for both generations for the second consecutive year.

More than half of Gen Zs (55%) and millennials (52%) report delaying major life decisions - such as marriage,
starting a family, starting a business, or furthering education - due to their financial situation.

Despite these pressures, the survey found real optimism: 53% of Gen Zs and 45% of millennials expect their
personal financial situation to improve within the next year.
