# Culture & Belonging Initiatives

Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) are open to all employees and meet monthly; new groups can be proposed
through the internal culture portal with 5+ interested employees, and approved groups receive a small
budget for events and activities.

New team members are paired with a peer buddy for their first 90 days, separate from their formal manager,
specifically to support social integration and answering informal questions that don't need to go through
official channels.

Quarterly team-culture surveys are anonymous and results are shared back with teams within 2 weeks,
with each team required to identify at least one concrete action based on the feedback.

The company hosts quarterly all-hands social events, both in-person (for office-based teams) and virtual
(for remote employees), intended to build cross-team relationships outside of pure work context.

Remote and hybrid employees have access to a dedicated "Remote Connect" budget for co-working space
memberships or occasional team meetups, recognizing that belonging looks different without a shared office.

Employees who feel disconnected from their immediate team can request a temporary rotation onto a
different project or team for a defined period, without it being treated as a performance signal -
sometimes a change of environment genuinely helps.

The company's stated culture principles - psychological safety, direct feedback, and inclusive
decision-making - are reinforced through mandatory manager training, updated annually based on
employee survey feedback.
