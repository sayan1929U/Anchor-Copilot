# Deloitte 2026 Gen Z & Millennial Survey — Purpose & Belonging Findings

According to Deloitte's 2026 Global Gen Z and Millennial Survey, purpose and a strong sense of belonging -
fostered specifically through workplace friendships - are described as non-negotiable factors in job
satisfaction and retention for both Gen Z and millennial employees.

The survey frames this generation's overall approach as a shift from "career acceleration" to "career
sustainability" - employees are choosing what is sustainable and meaningful over what is simply fast or
performative, and organizations that build genuine belonging are positioned to retain talent long-term.
