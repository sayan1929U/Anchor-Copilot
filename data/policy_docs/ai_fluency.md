# AI Tools & Readiness Guidelines

The company provides licensed access to approved AI coding and writing assistants for all employees;
access requests go through IT via the internal tools portal, typically approved within 2 business days.

AI literacy workshops are held monthly, covering practical use of AI tools in day-to-day work across
departments - no prior technical background required, and sessions are recorded for those who can't attend live.

Employees are not required to disclose AI tool usage for drafting internal documents, but should apply
judgment and review AI-assisted work before sharing externally or with clients.

On the question of job security and AI: the company's public position, reaffirmed by leadership in recent
town halls, is that AI tools are intended to augment employee capability and remove repetitive work, not
to justify headcount reduction. Roles are evaluated based on business needs, not AI adoption rates, and
any restructuring decisions go through standard HR processes with advance notice, not silent automation.

Teams are encouraged to identify repetitive, low-judgment tasks in their workflow that could reasonably be
automated with AI tools, freeing up time for higher-value work - employees who do this are recognized
positively in performance reviews as a sign of adaptability, not penalized.

The company offers a self-paced "AI Fundamentals" track covering prompt engineering, understanding AI
limitations, and responsible use - completion is optional but is tracked as a positive signal for employees
interested in roles with more AI-tooling responsibility.

Employees with specific anxiety about AI's impact on their function are encouraged to raise this directly
with their manager or HR business partner - this is treated as a legitimate, common concern, not an
overreaction, and managers are trained to have this conversation constructively.
