# Career Pathways & Promotion Framework

Promotion cycles run twice yearly, in March and September. Employees are eligible to be considered after
12 months in their current role, though exceptions exist for high-impact contributions or critical business needs.

Leadership development track: employees can opt into the "Rising Leaders" 6-month cohort program, which
includes mentorship pairing with a senior leader, a stretch project with real business impact, and
quarterly check-ins with senior leadership. Applications open each January and July.

Managers are expected to hold career-development conversations with direct reports at least twice yearly,
separate from standard performance reviews - these are meant to focus purely on growth, not evaluation.

Lateral moves between departments are actively supported; employees interested in switching functions can
use the internal mobility portal to express interest, and hiring managers are encouraged to prioritize
internal candidates before external recruiting for open roles.

For individual contributors who don't want to move into people management, the company maintains a parallel
"Technical/Principal track" with equivalent compensation bands to management roles at the same seniority level,
recognizing that leadership growth doesn't always mean managing people.

Employees can request a formal skip-level meeting with their manager's manager once per quarter to discuss
career trajectory, concerns, or opportunities that might not surface in regular 1:1s.

External conference attendance and industry certifications relevant to an employee's growth path can be
proposed to a manager for approval as part of the annual learning stipend, and are viewed favorably
during promotion consideration as evidence of growth investment.
