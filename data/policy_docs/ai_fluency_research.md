# Deloitte 2026 Gen Z & Millennial Survey — AI & the Readiness Gap Findings

According to Deloitte's 2026 Global Gen Z and Millennial Survey, AI adoption is now mainstream: 74% of
both Gen Zs and millennials report using AI in their day-to-day work, a sharp increase from 57% of Gen Zs
and 56% of millennials just one year earlier.

There is a clear "AI readiness gap" between employees and their organizations: nearly one-third of
respondents (30% of Gen Zs, 31% of millennials) believe their organization is not prepared for the changes
AI will bring, and roughly one-third (33% of Gen Zs, 32% of millennials) say the AI tools available to them
at work are only somewhat or not at all sufficient.

Beyond productivity, employees are using AI for personal support: 72% of Gen Zs and 69% of millennials use
AI to seek career advice, and 67% of Gen Zs and 65% of millennials use AI specifically to cope with
work-related stress.

Digital overload is a real, measured risk: 58% of Gen Zs and 54% of millennials report experiencing digital
fatigue from constant alerts, tool switching, and juggling multiple platforms.

Deloitte's research frames AI's impact on entry-level work positively: employees cite accelerated skill
learning, more time for higher-value work, and faster career growth potential as the leading effects of
AI adoption for early-career employees, not job displacement.
