# Recognition & Wellbeing Program

Peer recognition can be given anytime through the internal "Kudos" platform, visible to the whole team
and factored into quarterly recognition awards, which include a small monetary bonus and public acknowledgment.

The Employee Assistance Program offers confidential counseling sessions - up to 6 per year, covering
stress, burnout, and general mental health support, at no cost to the employee, available by phone, video,
or in person depending on location.

Managers receive quarterly reminders to check in on team wellbeing and are encouraged to flag any
signs of burnout early rather than waiting for a formal review cycle - this is tracked as a manager
responsibility, not optional.

The company observes "No Meeting Fridays" once a month company-wide, intended to give employees
uninterrupted focus time and reduce meeting fatigue, a direct response to employee feedback about burnout.

Annual "Recognition Week" includes team-nominated awards across categories like collaboration, innovation,
and mentorship, voted on by peers rather than assigned top-down by management.

Employees can request a confidential wellbeing check-in with an HR business partner (separate from their
manager) at any time, useful for situations where discussing burnout directly with a manager feels
uncomfortable.

Sabbatical leave is available after 5 years of continuous employment - up to 4 weeks of additional paid
leave intended specifically for rest and recovery, separate from standard vacation allocation.
