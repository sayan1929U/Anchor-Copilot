# Deloitte 2026 Gen Z & Millennial Survey — Leadership & Career Findings

According to Deloitte's 2026 Global Gen Z and Millennial Survey, only 6% of Gen Zs and millennials say
reaching a leadership position is their primary career goal - but this does not mean disengagement.
76% of Gen Zs and 67% of millennials remain interested in senior leadership roles at some point in their careers.

The most commonly cited barriers to pursuing leadership are stress and burnout (50% of Gen Zs, 49% of
millennials), excessive responsibility (50% of Gen Zs, 48% of millennials), and work-life balance concerns
(41% of Gen Zs, 46% of millennials).

Most respondents favor steady, gradual progress over rapid promotions: 44% of Gen Zs and 45% of millennials
prefer steady growth, compared to only 25% of Gen Zs and 21% of millennials who prioritize fast promotions.
Around one-fifth of both generations are open to lateral moves to build long-term career resilience.
