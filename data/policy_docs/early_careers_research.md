According to Deloitte's 2026 Global Gen Z and Millennial Survey, AI is reshaping entry-level work in ways
employees see as largely positive: respondents cite AI enabling entry-level employees to gain experience
faster (26% of Gen Zs, 28% of millennials), freeing them to focus on higher-value work (25% of both
generations), and accelerating career growth potential (25% of both generations).

The survey also flags a knowledge-continuity risk relevant to early-career employees joining teams with
long-tenured experts: only 54% of Gen Zs and 60% of millennials say their team could maintain performance
if a key expert left tomorrow, underscoring the importance of structured onboarding and mentorship for
new hires to absorb institutional knowledge early.
